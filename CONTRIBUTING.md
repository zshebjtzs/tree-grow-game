# Contributing

Thank you for considering a contribution to TreeGrow: Graph Theory.

## Before submitting

- For substantial changes, open an issue first to discuss the intended behavior.
- Keep changes focused and include or update automated tests where practical.
- Do not submit code, text, images, audio, fonts, or other material unless you have the rights needed to contribute it.
- Identify any third-party material and its original source and license.

## Pull requests

Please describe:

1. What changed and why.
2. How the change was tested.
3. Any compatibility, performance, or gameplay consequences.
4. Any third-party material added and its license.

A maintainer may ask for clarification about provenance or licensing before merging a contribution.
